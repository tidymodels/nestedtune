# M69: A `select` argument on the five orchestrators takes a `selection_rule()`, and the final fit applies the recorded rule

**Status:** done (2026-09-06, PR #79 https://github.com/tidymodels/nestedtune/pull/79)

**Goal:** Let the caller choose the rule each outer fold selects its candidate by, from tune's three selectors, recorded in the procedure so the final fit applies the same rule.

**Outcome:** `selection_rule(rule = c("best", "one_std_err", "pct_loss"), ..., limit = NULL)` in `R/selection-rule.R` returns a classed list holding the rule name, the orderings captured as bare expressions (`rlang::enexprs()`, no environment on the wire or in the fixture key), and `limit` (2 by default for `"pct_loss"`, `NULL` otherwise); it refuses a rule outside the three (`rlang_error`), a non-default rule with no ordering (`nestedtune_selection_rule_no_order`), an ordering with `"best"` or one that is a literal rather than a symbol or call (`nestedtune_selection_rule_order`), a bad `limit` (`nestedtune_selection_rule_limit`) and a named dot (`rlib_error_dots_named`); `new_selection_rule()`, `is_selection_rule()`, one-line `format()`/`print()`. `select = selection_rule("best")` follows `eval_time` on the five orchestrator signatures; `check_selection_rule()` in `R/checks.R` refuses a non-rule (`nestedtune_bad_selection_rule`) and an ordering naming a symbol outside the tuned parameter ids (`nestedtune_selection_rule_unknown_param`) at entry, no fold run. The rule travels through `nested_loop()`, `dispatch_folds()`, `fold_task()` to `nested_fold_fit()`, where `apply_selection_rule()` calls `tune::select_best()`, `select_by_one_std_err()` or `select_by_pct_loss()` with the first metric name and the orderings spliced; a selector returning no row fails the fold with a note naming the rule (`nestedtune_selection_rule_empty`). `new_procedure()` records `select`, `procedure_tuner()` lists it as shared, and `nested_final_fit()` applies the recorded rule; `check_results_record()` refuses a record lacking it as `nestedtune_bad_results`. Help on the six pages with a "Selected by `select`" paragraph and annotated by-hand recipes, `selection_rule()`'s page with an executed example, pkgdown row, NEWS, DESIGN's orchestration line, `tuners.Rmd` and `nested-cv.Rmd`. Tests: `test-selection-rule.R`; AC1 oracle blocks in the four `*-oracles.R` files through `reference_select()`; AC4 refusals in the five `*-checks.R` files; final-fit, extract-procedure and record-refusal tests; BC15 in `test-parallel-identity.R` (`pct_loss`, `limit = 5`, two daemons identical to serial).

**Decisions:** D-056 (constructor object over a closure or three formals; the `select` name; the entry check narrowing orderings to tuned parameter ids; a pre-rule record refused rather than migrated). Milestone-local: an empty selection fails the fold with a note rather than an entry-time refusal; a one-line `print()` method; the missing-rule refusal inside `check_results_record()`. Observed on tune 2.1.0: `select_by_pct_loss()` warns "numerical expression has 2 elements" when two rows tie at the best, surfaced once per fold and not silenced.
