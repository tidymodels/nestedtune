# M096: The help pages inherit tune's argument text and share repeated text through templates

**Status:** done (2026-09-15, PR #109 https://github.com/tidymodels/nestedtune/pull/109; resolves #91 partial)

**Goal:** Every `@param` on the six loop pages whose accepted values equal the wrapped tune, finetune or workflowsets argument's carries that package's own text, with the nested-CV qualification moved to the page's Details, and no roxygen prose line is repeated across blocks where a template or inheritance can carry it.

**Outcome:** `cairn/surveys/M096-param-survey.md` records 24 `@param` tags at the branch point, 5 `inherit` (grid page `param_info` and `grid`, bayes `iter`, race `grid`, fit-resamples `metrics`) and 19 `local`, each local row naming the value one side accepts and the other refuses. The five tags are now `@inheritParams tune::tune_grid`, `tune::tune_bayes`, `finetune::tune_race_anova`, `tune::fit_resamples`; the removed qualifications sit in each page's Details, with `man-roxygen/details-param-info.R` pointing the bayes, race and sim-anneal pages at their "Finalizing a parameter range" section. Seventeen `man-roxygen/` templates carry the repeated runs: eight `differences-*` paragraphs (forced, settable, refused, refused-plain, passed-shared, finetune-version, kept, not-returned, inert) each opening its own `@section` under the page's title, `param-control-dots`, `param-event-level`, `param-eval-time`, `refusals-saved-run`, `seealso-orchestrator`, `seealso-reader`, plus `@inheritParams` for the reader family's `x`, `summarize` and `...`. The bayes and sim-anneal "Refused" paragraphs name no example, since tune 2.1.0 and finetune 1.3.0 refuse the coercions the old examples claimed. The AC3 duplicate-line command (12+ words seen in two roxygen blocks) exits silent. One NEWS bullet names the six pages and four arguments.

**Decisions:** the AC3 duplicate-line command lives in the milestone file's Decisions section (git holds it); a `--duplicates` sweep mode was not planned. Roxygen splices a `@template`'s own tags, so a plain-text template cannot sit inside a section; each Differences template carries its own `@section` tag, and a guard-only `@examplesIf` template is impossible for the same reason (the four workflow-set guard lines outside `example-set.R` stay, under the 12-word floor).

**Review:** three-lens fan-out. History lens 0 regressions; prior-review lens no prior-review evidence; diff-bug lens 11 findings. Fix now: the finetune-version note's own `VERSION_CTRL` variable (the race and sim-anneal `...` items had lost their package prefix), the `param_info` pointer on the three inheriting pages, the NEWS bullet naming the sim-anneal page, the kept paragraph's "Each reaches the outer fit as well as the inner run" clause, the bayes Details naming tune's early stop. Follow-up candidate row: the template-merge rendering warts (a split `\itemize`, orphan paragraphs after a template boundary, doubled blank lines). Rejected: `grid = 2.5` (AC1's rule reads the two texts, both say integer), "inner `tune_results`" for a race result (a subclass), the no-op second `@inheritParams`. At hygiene the M61 lesson on finalizing `param_info` and `min_n` for a ranger workflow was retired: the pages' "Finalizing a parameter range" section owns that content.
