# M132: Refuse an inner "Apparent" split that tune drops or a race misreads

**Status:** done (2026-09-30, PR #147 https://github.com/tidymodels/nestedtune/pull/147)

**Goal:** Tuning runs on an inner design only where tune and finetune read each of its splits the way the user built them (D-100).

**Outcome:** `R/checks.R` gained `misread_apparent_rows()` and `check_inner_apparent_ids()`. tune 2.1.0 leaves every split under the id "Apparent" out of its estimates. So in the inner loop they refuse such a split, except the apparent split of a `bootstraps()` or `group_bootstraps()` design. finetune 1.3.0 eliminates candidates on unsummarized metrics that keep that split. So `check_race_apparent()` and `holds_apparent_split()` refuse it under the two racers. `check_nested()` and `inner_resamples_from_split()` apply the first rule. The racers' entry and the pre-loop of `nested_workflow_map()` apply the race rule. `check_final_inner()` applies both to the design `nested_final_fit()` rebuilds, in `final_fit_worker()` before `run_tuner()`. All refusals use class `nestedtune_bad_design`. The README, `?nested_resamples` and the "Nested designs" help state the rules. So do the racers' page, the final fit's "What is refused", NEWS and the DESIGN convention bullet. `test-design-support.R` and `test-nested-final-fit-checks.R` gained the tests.

**Decisions:** D-100, recorded at plan. D-101, recorded at review, corrects D-100's list of which rule each site applies.
