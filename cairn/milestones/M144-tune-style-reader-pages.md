## Goal

Rewrite the 24 help pages that M142 and M143 leave to the target M142 sets.

## Scope

**In:** the roxygen and the `man-roxygen/` templates behind the 24 other `man/*.Rd` pages. The eight pages left out are `nested_tune_grid`, `nested_tune_bayes`, `nested_tune_race`, `nested_tune_sim_anneal`, `nested_fit_resamples`, `nested_workflow_map`, `nested_final_fit` and `nested_resamples`. Tests that read the text of these pages, rewritten to the new pages. The figures in `cairn/references/tune-docs-comparison.md`. A guide sentence that points to text these pages drop.

**Out:**

- The eight loop and design pages are M142's and M143's.
- The guides go to M145.
- `reexports.Rd` is generated by roxygen and is measured but not rewritten.

**Target:** the M142 target, read from `cairn/references/tune-docs-comparison.md`. The pages here are close to tune's mean length already (9,215 words over 24 pages against tune's 360 a page). The gap is long Descriptions, refusal lists, condition class names, the standard-error argument and the pointers between sections.
